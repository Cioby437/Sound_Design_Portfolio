WHAT I DO

SOUND DESIGN

Custom sound effects for games, trailers and animation — layered, processed and shaped so every action on screen has a voice of its own.

SOUND REDESIGN

Stripping existing footage down to silence and rebuilding its entire soundscape — a fresh take on the story told through audio.

INTERACTIVE AUDIO

Audio that reacts to the player — adaptive ambiences, state-driven music and feedback sounds designed around gameplay.

AUDIO IMPLEMENTATION

Getting sounds into the game and working as intended — FMOD events, parameters and Unity integration in C#.

MIXING
